WHO WE ARE

A Kenyan Story of
Growth and Opportunity.

Zedgee Limited is a Kenyan-owned and locally managed flower growing and export company incorporated in 2003.

Founded with a vision of connecting Kenyan flower growers to international markets, we bring together local growing expertise, coordinated sourcing and export operations.

Our approach combines flowers grown on our farms with flowers sourced from partner growers across Kenya, creating a diverse selection for customers around the world.

OUR JOURNEY

Growing Beyond
Boundaries.

Our Beginning

Established in 2003, Zedgee Limited was founded with a vision of creating opportunities for Kenyan flower growers to access international markets.

Building Partnerships

Through relationships with local growers, we developed a coordinated sourcing approach that brings together different flower varieties from across Kenya.

International Connections

Our flowers reach customers in international markets including the USA, Holland, France and the Middle East.

Looking Ahead

We continue to pursue opportunities to expand our flower range and strengthen partnerships with Kenyan growers to serve customer needs.

OUR OPERATIONS

Local Expertise.
International Reach.

Our operations bring together growing, sourcing, grading and international flower marketing.

01 /

Flower Farms

Our farms are spread across Kiambu and Nyandarua counties in Kenya. We also work with local growers to source a wider selection of flowers.

02 /

Grading & Processing

Our flower grading and processing facility is based in Limuru, Kiambu County, approximately 30 kilometres from Nairobi.

03 /

Global Marketing

Our Nairobi head office supports our operations, while our Amsterdam marketing representation connects us to the international flower trade.
